# Understand Robocup Conceptually
The **purpose** of this note is having a **god-mode** view of **robocup** instead of boring codes, class diagram or API that didn't tell the complete story. Hence while reading this notes, you are recommended to use a **listen-to-story** mode to understand problem statement.

## Background

Robocup competition is inspired by [1995 Great Hanshin Earthquake ↗](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Hanshin_earthquake). That's **1995** guys, imagine when disaster happened, phone infrastructure destroyed. The only communication tool is [Two-way radio ↗](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-way_radio).

When resident was buried, everywhere is fired. Ambulance need to rescue refugee, firefighter need to extinguish fire, police need to clean the road by removing blockades so ambulance and firefighter can travel to any location.

## Agent Worldview

Imagine you are police/paramedic/firefighter with one two-way radio to communicate. You can hear a "Help ..." from refugee, but as a paramedic, you can't go to your target destination because of **blockade**, as a police, you dont have ambulance surrounding you when someone injured seriously, as a firefighter, you don't know how to rescue a seriously injured refugee. What you have, is the **map of city** in your mind, is the single **two-way radio** in your pocket, is the **view** of what you can see.

In the scenario above, what can you do ? You will immediately call your station, report what you see, report your current location, report the problem faced. You should wait for station to send someone to solve the problem. 

As a **resident**, your phone won't work when disaster happened because all the communication infrastructure was destroyed, you lose your house because your house was destroyed under an earthquake, you will probably **buried** because the building fall down too fast, even worst that you are **heavily injured**, you just act like the lamb to the slaughter moaning in pain(Help...), praying someone can find you. Even you are lucky that you are not buried and you are not injured seriously, but you have to walk to the refuge stations to get food or drinks to survive because transportation destroyed so you can't drive, no one know whether the second wave disaster would happen or not when you walk to station, no one know whether the explosion will happen or not when you are near the gas station. Note that second wave won't stimulate in robocup.

As a **paramedic**, you should know how to **rescue** patient, you should know how to use the two-way radio to receive **command** sent from station, you should know how to **communicate** with station. If your path have been blocked, you have 2 options, either you choose another path or wait for police to clean the blockade. Station should give the answer because only station have the worldview of all the dynamic state of the city. 

As a **firefighter**, you should know how to **extinguish the fire**, you should keep **communication** with the station. You will face the same problem with ambulance also, blockage. You can also have 2 solution, choose another path or wait for station. However, there's 2 more variables firefighter need to concern. Let's recall what is the 3 elements that produce a fire, oxygen, heat, fuel. We can't control the oxygen because the oxygen is staying in atmosphere which is everywhere, but how about fuel and heat? Imagine you sit inside fire truck, looking outside, you knew the building is built with what materials, wood/steel/concrete, you take note the every material and thinking which possible to be the fuel. The largest fuel available in a city is **gas station**, there are thousands of black liquid stored in a gas station, a little spark can cause the surrounding within a radius of multi-kilometer on fire. As a firefighter, you need to isolate the fuel and heat. You need to make sure the gas station surrounding area always in low **temperature**, you need a "heat map" that keep in your mind. 

As a **police**, you should know how to clean **blockade**, you should know how to use the two-way radio to receive the command from station, you should know how to report the problem faced. If you see someone injured seriously, you should report location and situation. If you see somewhere fired, you should report location and situation also. You are the people who make sure the **blood vessel** of the city won't blocked, paramedic can't save a life when blockage is the mountain stopping him to move on, firefighter can only watch the fire to burn everything even he have the water because blockage is blocking the road. 

**Station**, this is where control the agents and the entire city. If agents is the eye that give station the worldview, then station is the **brain** that keep track all the dynamic state in the city. Where have fire, where is the location of each agent, which task have the highest priority, how many agent needed to solve a task, and many many questions wait to answer. What you have is the **pieces** of the **jigsaw puzzle** and you should interpret and chain all these together.

## Communication

Based on the background, we roughly know what is the limitations and have a view on real situation. In robocup simulation, how communication happened ? See, any action implemented should work on something important, firefighter are not going to somewhere dont have fire, police should go clean blockage instead of simply walk around, ambulance should immediately go to someone who is in danger instead of helping a non-serious refugee. Every resources should be allocated on correct point accurately. The only communication tool you have is **two-way radio**, it's not one-to-one like you call to your friend, it's **broadcast**. You press the button to say something like "Here is ID123, talk to ID456 ...", when you release button, you can hear everyone is busy talking or shouting. 

Police have police office, ambulance have ambulance centre, firefighter have fire centre. All agent is highly centralised and execute commands sent from centre. While all agents is the **eye** of centre that draw a full picture.

If we denote **agent and station** as a **point** and we draw a **line** between 2 point that represent the **two-way-communication**, then we can get a "sea urchin" shape of communication map by default behavior. Every agent is only communicate with station and they selectively ignore the message from peer. I am not saying this is bad, but can we have a communication map like a "net" ? I am talking about agent-to-agent version of Inter-Process Communication(IPC), or I can call it as **Inter-Agent Communication**. Station is busy playing around the jigsaw puzzle, if station is stuck and agent decide to wait command but no command received, agent can't busy waiting. So as an agent, you have to use your brain, you should quickly pickup all the available resources surrounding you when station is not responding, you should **communicate with agents surrounding** you to get information, you should make your own decision when station is busy and not responding, you should ask for help from surrounding agent to get task instead of asking from centre. Report to station only when station request or it's important, not something can be solved by inter-agent communication. Another benefits of inter-agent communication is **avoid repetitive task**. You don't need 3 ambulance to rescue one refugee, that's not "making him safer" but it's a waste of resources.
